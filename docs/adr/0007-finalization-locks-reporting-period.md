# Finalizing a VAT Report locks its Reporting Period

Users and Administrators can approve Documents, but only an Administrator can finalize a VAT Report. Finalization puts a Period Lock on the Reporting Period: its Documents cannot be edited, re-approved or deleted. Only an Administrator can unlock the period, and the unlock is audited. Approved Documents in a locked period cannot be deleted at all, because Czech VAT law requires 10-year retention. Corrective (supplementary) returns are out of MVP scope; until they are supported, the workaround is to unlock, correct and export again. iVAT does not submit to the tax authority. It exports EPO XML (DPHDP3, DPHKH1), validated against the official XSD.

A newly uploaded Document whose Extraction indicates a locked Reporting Period is not yet part of that period. Its Extraction is preserved and it proceeds to review with a Period Lock warning; approval or assignment into the locked Reporting Period remains blocked until an Administrator unlocks it. The same observation rule applies to an explicitly accepted AI rerun whose current Reporting Period remains unlocked: only the worker completion bound to its current request and owned allowance reservation may append that successful AI Extraction for review. It does not approve the Document or alter the locked report; manual corrections and subsequent reruns remain subject to the current-period lock.

ADR-0014 extends Finalization and explicit unlocking to SuperAdmin without a Membership. The same confirmations, audit and Period Lock safeguards apply; the platform Role does not bypass them.

# Monthly KH for a quarterly legal person: the month-selection rule remains unverified

Verified **13 November 2026** for [#277](https://github.com/Thomisso/iVAT/issues/277). Scope: ordinary domestic incoming invoices, full deduction, KH B.2/B.3, a legal person with quarterly VAT returns. This is evidence for the existing factual gate, not implementation, a new product decision, or professional confirmation. The issue body and all comments were read first; the existing [claim-deadline](claim-deadline.md) and [year-end](claim-deadline-new-yearend.md) notes address different questions.

## Result

**The additional primary sources narrow the gap but do not establish a deterministic allocation of ordinary incoming deductions among a quarterly legal person's three monthly KH.** Current statutory text and the replacement 2026 form decree were read directly, rather than relying on indexed snippets. They distinguish the quarterly deduction period, monthly KH period and supplier's tax point, but the examined provisions do not expressly say whether an intra-quarter month is fixed by receipt, VAT-record entry, another fact, or a bounded taxpayer election. No default to DUZP, upload, recording timestamp or quarter end is established by this research. [VAT Act §§ 73, 100, 101c–101e](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235/2026-01-01#par_73); [Decree 387/2025, Annex 12, B.2/B.3](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2025/387/2026-01-01#pril_12).

Two material additions to the prior evidence are **Decree 387/2025 effective 1 January 2026**, replacing the older form decree, and **FS's 13 April 2026 statement** about invoices received after period end but before filing. Neither supplies the missing KH month selector. [Decree §§ 5–7](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2025/387/2026-01-01#par_5); [FS statement](https://financnisprava.gov.cz/cs/dane/dane/dan-z-pridane-hodnoty/informace-stanoviska-a-sdeleni/narok-na-odpocet-dph/sdeleni-fscr-k-okamziku-uplat-narok-odp-dane).

## What the current primary sources establish

| Question | Source-supported answer and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Filing cadence | A legal person files KH for a calendar month within 25 days after it ends, including when VAT returns are quarterly. Natural persons instead follow the VAT-return filing period; their quarterly KH examples cannot establish allocation among three legal-person monthly KH. [VAT Act § 101e](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235/2026-01-01#par_101e); [FS methodology § 1.3, p. 3](https://financnisprava.gov.cz/assets/cs/prilohy/d-seznam-dani/Metodicka_informace_k_vyplneni_KH_20240101.pdf). |
| Entitlement versus exercise | Entitlement arises when the facts creating the obligation to declare that tax occur. Ordinary supplier-charged deduction requires possession of the tax document; the earliest claim is for the **taxable period** in which the conditions are fulfilled. For the stipulated quarterly payer that period is a quarter, not three hypothetical monthly VAT returns. [VAT Act §§ 72(5), 73(1)(a), 73(2), 99a](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235/2026-01-01#par_72-odst_5). |
| Later deduction period | GFŘ's example permits claiming ordinary full deduction in a later taxable period within the applicable limit. It expressly gives July/**Q3** as the earliest period for a May supply whose invoice arrives in July. This establishes quarter eligibility and postponement, not selection of July/August/September KH. [GFŘ information, § 3, example 2, p. 3](https://financnisprava.gov.cz/assets/cs/prilohy/d-seznam-dani/94497_Novela_2025_Informace_zmeny_naroku_odpocet_dane.pdf). |
| Actual exercise day | The same GFŘ section defines the day of exercising deduction as the day the return containing it is filed. **Inference:** using that real filing day's calendar month to select KH would confuse the act of filing with the period reported; the source gives no such algorithm. [GFŘ § 3, p. 3](https://financnisprava.gov.cz/assets/cs/prilohy/d-seznam-dani/94497_Novela_2025_Informace_zmeny_naroku_odpocet_dane.pdf). |
| Why ordinary inputs enter KH | Section 101c(b)(2) concerns deduction of supplier-charged tax; § 101d(1)(c) requires deduction data. Annex 12 B.2 associates the invoice with deduction in the related return's taxable period; B.3 aggregates the relevant amounts for the month/period. This wording does not resolve intra-quarter month selection. [VAT Act §§ 101c–101d](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235/2026-01-01#par_101c); [current Annex 12, B.2/B.3](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2025/387/2026-01-01#pril_12). |
| DPPD | Current Annex 12 B.2 defines DPPD by the supplier's obligation to declare tax, referring to entitlement under § 72(5). It does not define DPPD as invoice receipt, upload, approval, or chosen deduction/KH month. [Annex 12, B.2, DPPD item](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2025/387/2026-01-01#pril_12). |
| Recording | Section 100(1)(a) requires data in the detail needed to prepare returns/KH; § 100(5)(a) requires proper recording by the applicable filing deadline. **Inference:** a recording duty/deadline alone does not make the physical data-entry timestamp the KH allocation month. The examined text gives no such equation. [VAT Act § 100](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235/2026-01-01#par_100). |
| Reconciliation | Each KH C sums that particular KH's A/B data; the three monthly KH reconcile to the associated quarterly return. An aggregate reconciliation does not determine the unique monthly distribution. [Current Annex 12, C](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2025/387/2026-01-01#pril_12); [FS FAQ XV/1–2](https://financnisprava.gov.cz/cs/dane/dane/dan-z-pridane-hodnoty/kontrolni-hlaseni-dph/caste-dotazy-a-odpovedi). |

### Court evidence does not create a positive algorithm

[NSS 7 Afs 317/2024-23, 13 May 2025, paragraphs 17 and 26](https://vyhledavac.nssoud.cz/DokumentOriginal/Html/741531) rejects proving a June KH obligation merely from a quarterly return and missing incoming amounts in April/May KH. It treats the actual relevant month as an evidentiary gap, not an automatic June remainder. That does not establish free allocation, compulsory receipt-month allocation, or a universal ban on choosing the last month.

The underlying [Městský soud 10 Af 21/2023-40, 21 November 2024, paragraphs 28–33](https://vyhledavac.nssoud.cz/DokumentOriginal/Index/737249) is particularly explicit in paragraph 33: it does not prejudge whether the supplies were actually received in May **or whether the subsequent May KH complied with the law**. Treating that May filing as judicial approval of an allocation rule would go beyond the decision. These decisions address a penalty and proof of duty, not the hypothetical January/March choice in #277.

### Freshness: current forms and receipt after period end

[Decree 387/2025](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2025/387/2026-01-01), dated **25 September 2025**, promulgated **9 October 2025**, effective **1 January 2026**, places KH instructions in **Annex 12** (§ 2(1)(l), (3)). Section 6 repeals Decree 457/2020; § 5 retains the prior decree for tax statements for periods beginning before commencement. The [FS KH download index](https://financnisprava.gov.cz/cs/dane/dane/dan-z-pridane-hodnoty/kontrolni-hlaseni-dph/soubory-ke-stazeni), read on the verification date, still leads with methodology effective 1 January 2024, published 22 February 2024, and instructions published 28 April 2023. They are useful dated guidance, not sufficient proof of the currently binding decree.

[FS's statement of **13 April 2026**](https://financnisprava.gov.cz/cs/dane/dane/dan-z-pridane-hodnoty/informace-stanoviska-a-sdeleni/narok-na-odpocet-dph/sdeleni-fscr-k-okamziku-uplat-narok-odp-dane) specifically retains earliest deduction in the following taxable period when the invoice arrives after the supply's period but before its return is filed. It explains that the contrary [General Court T-689/24 judgment of **11 February 2026**, operative part published **13 April 2026**](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/2034/oj/eng) is subject to review and its answer has not taken effect pending that review. Distinguish this administrative position from a new final Court of Justice holding.

The [Court of Justice review decision, C-167/26 RX, **26 March 2026**, published **15 June 2026**](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/3045) identifies exactly that receipt-before-filing issue. [AG Kokott's opinion of **10 September 2026**, paragraph 88](https://juris.curia.europa.eu/juris/document/document.jsf?docid=315069&doclang=EN) proposes requiring the invoice in the deduction period; it is an opinion, not the final judgment. This bounded search found no later final review judgment effective by **13 November 2026**. Recheck that proceeding before implementation; do not turn the February judgment or September opinion into an implemented exception. None of these sources decides ordinary incoming allocation among monthly Czech KH within one quarterly deduction period.

## The six acceptance cases: quarter support versus missing month support

These are **derived illustrations**, not reported taxpayer cases. Assume all other ordinary full-deduction conditions are met, no special correction/reverse charge/advance, no prior claim, and a timely claim. “Earliest” does not require claiming immediately. Quarter conclusions apply the current § 73 rule and FS's stated practice; the post-period-receipt rows retain the EU-review qualification above. [VAT Act § 73](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235/2026-01-01#par_73); [GFŘ § 3, example 2](https://financnisprava.gov.cz/assets/cs/prilohy/d-seznam-dani/94497_Novela_2025_Informace_zmeny_naroku_odpocet_dane.pdf); [FS 13 April 2026](https://financnisprava.gov.cz/cs/dane/dane/dan-z-pridane-hodnoty/informace-stanoviska-a-sdeleni/narok-na-odpocet-dph/sdeleni-fscr-k-okamziku-uplat-narok-odp-dane).

| Facts / confirmed deduction | Supported quarter conclusion | KH question still requiring direct support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| January 2026 supply and receipt; claim Q1 | Q1 is the earliest eligible quarter. | Is January required, or may March be chosen? What contemporaneous VAT-record fact/election controls? |
| January supply; invoice received March 2026; claim Q1 | Q1 remains the earliest taxable period. | Are January/February forbidden despite being in the eligible quarter? March-only cannot be proved merely by substituting “month” into § 73(2). |
| March supply; invoice received April 2026 | Earliest Q2 under the cited domestic rule/practice; Q1 is not supported by that practice. | Which Q2 month, and what proves it? March DUZP alone supplies no affirmative March KH algorithm. |
| December 2025 supply; received January 2026 | Earliest Q1/2026 under that rule/practice; prior Q4 is not supported by it. | January versus another Q1 month remains unanswered; DPPD retains the supplier's tax point. |
| January 2026 supply and receipt; deduction voluntarily deferred to Q2 | Later-quarter deduction is supported within the applicable statutory limit. | Does receipt, a recorded election, or another fact select April/May/June? A Q1 receipt alone cannot identify the Q2 month. |
| Supply 31 March 2026; receipt 10 April; Q1 return filed 20 April | Under FS's expressly addressed scenario, receipt before filing does not make Q1 the earliest eligible quarter; earliest Q2, subject to the review qualification. | The April receipt still does not prove a complete April-only KH allocation rule for this quarterly payer. |

The [FS FAQ VIII/4](https://financnisprava.gov.cz/cs/dane/dane/dan-z-pridane-hodnoty/kontrolni-hlaseni-dph/caste-dotazy-a-odpovedi), published 19 January 2016, and [FS answer 118](https://app.fs.gov.cz/dotazy-verejnosti/dotaz/narok-na-odpocet-dane-z-prijate-faktury-%E2%80%93-prislusne-zdanovaci-obdobi-1663), published 16 December 2025, give monthly-period examples. They do not answer the intra-quarter cells. The FAQ's older three-year wording is not current authority for post-2024 entitlement; use the separate deadline research and current § 73 instead.

## Precisely what is missing

**Question for a primary-source or written professional legal interpretation, not a product preference:** For a legal person whose VAT taxable period is Q1 and which claims an ordinary full supplier-charged deduction for Q1, what legal fact or permitted election fixes the monthly B.2/B.3 KH—particularly January receipt with proposed March KH, and March receipt with proposed January KH? If an election exists, what are its earliest-month limit, required VAT-record entry and timing, and consequences of postponement/previous KH filings? Please identify the controlling provision/guidance and answer the six rows above. A human confirmation is evidence of a decision; it cannot supply the missing legal validity bounds.

Until answered, the evidence needed for review should distinguish: supplier tax point/entitlement fact; actual tax-document receipt and its proof; earliest eligible **quarter**; chosen deduction quarter; any existing monthly VAT-record attribution/KH history and its basis; and the proposed KH month with the confirming person's reason. This is a research-derived evidence checklist, **not** a claim that § 100 prescribes these exact database fields. Recording an invoice in the software and choosing its tax period are separate assertions; neither should be silently substituted for the other. [VAT Act §§ 72(5), 73, 100](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235/2026-01-01#par_100); [current Annex 12](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2025/387/2026-01-01#pril_12).

This note does not complete #277's separate output/self-assessment mapping gate. The ordinary-input condition in § 73(1)(a) is distinct from § 73(1)(b); extending these deductions' timing to output obligations would require the applicable regime's own rule. [VAT Act § 73(1)](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235/2026-01-01#par_73-odst_1).

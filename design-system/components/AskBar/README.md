# AskBar

A question box for the open Document or Reporting Period, with a cited answer; pass `placeholder`, `answer` and `citations`.

- Lives at the top of the review panel and on the period overview. `⌘K` focuses it.
- Answers are short, plain and cite where they came from: a page of a Source File, or a Document number. A citation chip opens that place.
- The AI answers questions; it does not change data from here. Changes go through AiSuggestion or the fields.
- Placeholder suggests a real question: "Why is this reverse charge?", "Which invoices from Q3 are missing a Tax Point Date?"
